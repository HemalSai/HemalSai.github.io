02 / FEATURED RESEARCH

MASTER'S THESIS · 2026

Adversarial Attacks on AI-Powered Cyber Defense Systems and Their Impact on Digital Forensics

Research question: How does adversarial behaviour affect the reliability of AI-assisted detection and the completeness, temporal coherence and correlation of downstream forensic evidence?

01

Experimental platform

Built a segmented testbed connecting attack generation, application services, network sensors, machine-learning analysis and centralized security monitoring.

02

Adversarial scenarios

Evaluated reconnaissance, low-rate scanning, authentication anomalies, SQL injection and application-level manipulation under controlled conditions.

03

Evidence reliability

Examined gaps between observed activity, AI-generated alerts and records available for incident reconstruction across multiple layers.

Methods & tooling

03 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Future research directions.

Hardware & implementation security

How do hardware design, firmware and implementation details influence system security, including side-channel and fault-related risks?

Embedded systems security

How can security properties be evaluated across resource-constrained devices, firmware, interfaces and connected components?

Cyber-physical systems security

How do attacks on computing and communication components affect safety, reliability and trust in systems interacting with the physical world?

Systems & adversarial security

How can reproducible experiments uncover failures across software, networks, identity, trust boundaries and security monitoring?

These are prospective doctoral research interests. My demonstrated research experience is in adversarial AI-assisted cyber defence, networked systems and forensic evidence evaluation.
